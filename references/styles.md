# Style Library

54 named styles in 10 families: the 50 from Himanshu Bhardwaj's "50 Design Styles Every Designer Should Know for Better Prompting" (UX Planet, Aug 2025, https://uxplanet.org/50-design-styles-every-designer-should-know-for-better-prompting-56c09d55db62), plus 4 additions (Minimalism, Swiss Style, Flat Design, Claymorphism). The descriptions below are written fresh for prompting, not quoted from the source.

Each entry:

- **DNA**: palette · type · texture · layout · signature motifs. Feeds the prompt's Style and Palette & Materials lines.
- **Mood**: the feeling it gives off.
- **Fits**: image types (as named in `image-types.md`) where it lands well.
- **Prompt**: a keyword string to drop into the Style line.
- **Misfire**: how it typically goes wrong; feeds the Avoid line.
- **Pairs** / **Clashes**: guardrails for combinations.

Families, for spreading recommendations: Classical & Ornate · Dreamy & Ethereal · Retro-Digital · Bold & Loud · Structured & Functional · Calm & Natural · Dark & Dramatic · Handmade & Collage · Rustic & Heritage · Cute & Character.

## Classical & Ornate

### Neoclassical
- **DNA:** ivory, stone grey, muted gold · high-contrast Roman serifs, generous tracking · marble, plaster relief · strict symmetry, centered axis · columns, laurel wreaths, busts, pediments.
- **Mood:** formal, timeless, authoritative.
- **Fits:** Brand, Poster, Document, Product (heritage luxury).
- **Prompt:** `neoclassical design, symmetrical composition, marble and plaster textures, Roman serif typography, laurel and column motifs, muted ivory and gold palette`
- **Misfire:** turns into a cheap toga-party look or a museum-ticket template; too much gold.
- **Pairs:** Luxury Typography, Minimalism, Light Academia · **Clashes:** Graffiti, Kawaii, Cybercore.

### Baroque
- **DNA:** deep crimson, oxblood, black, burnished gold · ornate display serifs · gilded frames, velvet, heavy shadow · dense, theatrical, diagonal movement · scrolls, cherubs, drapery, mythic figures.
- **Mood:** opulent, emotional, grand.
- **Fits:** Poster (invitations, events), Product (premium packaging), Illustration, Scene.
- **Prompt:** `baroque style, dramatic chiaroscuro, ornate gilded scrollwork, rich crimson velvet and burnished gold, theatrical diagonal composition`
- **Misfire:** visual noise with no focal point; reads as a fake old-master pastiche.
- **Pairs:** Tenebrism, Gothic, Luxury Typography · **Clashes:** Bento Box, Flat Design, Utilitarian.

### Filigree
- **DNA:** gold or silver on cream, navy, or black · monogram and engraved scripts · fine metallic linework, foil · framed, centered, border-led · lace scrolls, interlaced curls, crests.
- **Mood:** delicate, ceremonial, precious.
- **Fits:** Poster (invitations, certificates), Product (luxury packaging), Brand (monograms).
- **Prompt:** `intricate filigree ornament, fine gold foil linework, lace-like scroll borders, engraved monogram typography, centered framed layout`
- **Misfire:** lines turn blobby or smeared at small sizes; borders swallow the content.
- **Pairs:** Art Nouveau, Luxury Typography, Light Academia · **Clashes:** Brutalism, Pixel Art, Graffiti.

### Acanthus
- **DNA:** sage, moss, carved-stone beige, gold · classical serifs · carved stone, relief · symmetrical, mirrored vines · acanthus leaves, scrolling foliage, Corinthian capitals.
- **Mood:** regal yet organic, enduring.
- **Fits:** Poster (wedding invites), Brand (museums, heritage), Product (premium).
- **Prompt:** `acanthus leaf ornament, carved stone relief, symmetrical scrolling foliage, sage green and antique gold, classical serif lettering`
- **Misfire:** leaves collapse into generic clip-art vines.
- **Pairs:** Neoclassical, Art Nouveau, Filigree · **Clashes:** Cybercore, Y2K, Memphis.

### Victorian
- **DNA:** burgundy, bottle green, mustard, gold · ornate wood-type and decorative serifs, mixed sizes · damask, embossed paper, gilt frames · busy, framed panels, centered · heavy florals, cameos, filigree corners.
- **Mood:** opulent, historical, romantic.
- **Fits:** Product (tea, apothecary labels), Poster, Document (book covers), Heritage.
- **Prompt:** `Victorian design, ornate decorative serif typography, damask patterns, gilded frames, deep burgundy and bottle green, heavy floral ornament`
- **Misfire:** cluttered type with too many fonts; muddy colors.
- **Pairs:** Steampunk, Gothic, Shabby Chic · **Clashes:** Bento Box, Glassmorphism, Neo Frutiger Aero.

### Art Deco
- **DNA:** black, gold, emerald, navy, ivory · tall geometric sans with stylized strokes · lacquer, brass, polished stone · strong symmetry, vertical rhythm · sunbursts, fans, stepped forms, chevrons.
- **Mood:** glamorous, jazz-age, confident.
- **Fits:** Poster, Brand, Product (spirits, fragrance), Space (lobby, bar).
- **Prompt:** `Art Deco style, symmetrical geometric composition, sunburst and chevron motifs, black and gold with emerald accents, stylized geometric sans-serif lettering`
- **Misfire:** generic "Gatsby party" clichés; geometry drawn wobbly.
- **Pairs:** Luxury Typography, Neoclassical, Tenebrism · **Clashes:** Wabi Sabi, Scrapbook, Kawaii.

### Art Nouveau
- **DNA:** olive, ochre, dusty rose, teal, cream · flowing hand-lettered type · stained-glass, lithograph flat color with outlines · organic frames, whiplash curves · lilies, vines, long-haired figures, peacock feathers.
- **Mood:** poetic, sensual, nature-bound.
- **Fits:** Poster, Product (natural cosmetics, tea), Illustration, Brand.
- **Prompt:** `Art Nouveau poster style, flowing whiplash curves, botanical frame, stained-glass outlines, muted olive ochre and dusty rose, hand-lettered title`
- **Misfire:** derivative Mucha copy; lettering illegible.
- **Pairs:** Filigree, Ethereal, Kawaii (soft) · **Clashes:** Brutalism, Utilitarian, Cybercore.

### Luxury Typography
- **DNA:** monochrome (black, ivory) with gold foil or one deep accent · high-contrast didone serifs, wide tracking, ligatures · paper, foil, emboss · vast negative space, type as the hero · minimal, often only a wordmark.
- **Mood:** elegant, exclusive, quiet power.
- **Fits:** Brand, Poster, Product (fashion, beauty, fragrance), Document (editorial).
- **Prompt:** `luxury typography, high-contrast didone serif, wide letter spacing, monochrome with gold foil accent, generous negative space, editorial fashion layout`
- **Misfire:** misspelled wordmark; reads empty rather than intentional.
- **Pairs:** Aurora, Minimalism, Art Deco · **Clashes:** Kitsch, Graffiti, Pixel Art.

## Dreamy & Ethereal

### Aurora
- **DNA:** teal, violet, magenta, deep night blue · thin modern sans · iridescent gradients, blur, glow · flowing, horizon-led or abstract field · northern-lights ribbons, soft waves.
- **Mood:** dreamy, futuristic, meditative.
- **Fits:** Poster, Product (tech, wellness), UI (backgrounds), Brand.
- **Prompt:** `aurora gradient, flowing iridescent ribbons of teal violet and magenta, soft glow and blur, deep night-blue background, airy thin sans-serif`
- **Misfire:** generic desktop wallpaper; mushy banding.
- **Pairs:** Glassmorphism, Luxury Typography, Minimalism · **Clashes:** Farmhouse / Cottagecore, Graffiti, Victorian.

### Ethereal
- **DNA:** pearl white, blush, lavender, pale gold · fine serifs or light script · gauze, mist, feathered shadow, low contrast · open and airy, floating elements · veils, petals, light rays.
- **Mood:** weightless, romantic, otherworldly.
- **Fits:** Photography (beauty, fashion), Product (skincare), Poster, Illustration.
- **Prompt:** `ethereal aesthetic, soft pastel haze, translucent veils and diffused light, low contrast, pearl blush and lavender tones, delicate thin serif`
- **Misfire:** washed out to the point of no subject; everything foggy.
- **Pairs:** Coquette, Art Nouveau, Light Academia · **Clashes:** Brutalism, Cybercore, Pop Art.

### Glassmorphism
- **DNA:** vivid gradient backdrop with frosted white panels, neon tints · clean geometric sans · frosted glass, background blur, thin light borders · layered floating cards · translucent panels, soft shadows.
- **Mood:** sleek, modern, layered.
- **Fits:** UI, Product (tech launches), Infographic.
- **Prompt:** `glassmorphism, frosted translucent glass panels with background blur, thin light borders, soft shadows, vivid gradient backdrop, clean geometric sans`
- **Misfire:** low-contrast text over busy blur; looks like a 2021 Dribbble shot.
- **Pairs:** Aurora, Neo Frutiger Aero, Bento Box · **Clashes:** Wabi Sabi, Farmhouse / Cottagecore, Brutalism.

### Coquette
- **DNA:** baby pink, cream, pearl, cherry red accents · dainty serifs, curly script · lace, satin, soft-focus filter · centered, framed, cozy close-ups · bows, ribbons, pearls, hearts, love letters.
- **Mood:** flirty, delicate, nostalgic-girly.
- **Fits:** Photography (lifestyle), Product (beauty), Poster (social covers), Illustration.
- **Prompt:** `coquette aesthetic, satin ribbon bows, pearls and lace, baby pink and cream palette, soft-focus dreamy filter, dainty serif and curly script`
- **Misfire:** saccharine overload; bows everywhere.
- **Pairs:** Ethereal, Shabby Chic, Scrapbook · **Clashes:** Utilitarian, Cybercore, Brutalism.

### Surrealism
- **DNA:** any palette, often clear skies and hard sunlight · minimal type · photoreal or painterly render · uncanny juxtaposition, impossible scale · melting forms, floating objects, doors in skies.
- **Mood:** dreamlike, uncanny, thought-provoking.
- **Fits:** Illustration, Poster (concept), Scene, Photography (conceptual).
- **Prompt:** `surrealist composition, impossible scale and dreamlike juxtaposition, crisp hard light, uncanny calm, painterly realism`
- **Misfire:** random weirdness with no single idea; too many visual metaphors.
- **Pairs:** Minimalism, Vaporwave, Tenebrism · **Clashes:** Bento Box, Utilitarian, Swiss Style.

## Retro-Digital

### Pixel Art
- **DNA:** limited palette (8–32 colors) · bitmap pixel fonts · visible square pixels, dithering · tile-based scenes, side or top-down view · sprites, hearts, coins, health bars.
- **Mood:** nostalgic, geeky, playful.
- **Fits:** Character, Scene, Illustration, Poster (game events).
- **Prompt:** `16-bit pixel art, crisp visible pixels, limited palette with dithering, side-view game scene, bitmap pixel font`
- **Misfire:** inconsistent pixel size or antialiased blur; "pixel filter" over a smooth image.
- **Pairs:** Y2K, Kawaii, Synthwave · **Clashes:** Filigree, Luxury Typography, Ethereal.

### Y2K
- **DNA:** chrome silver, icy blue, hot pink, lime · bubbly or pixel fonts, techno display · chrome, liquid metal, iridescent gloss · busy, stickered, starbursts · old browser windows, sparkles, butterflies, tribal swooshes, CD-ROM orbs.
- **Mood:** futuristic-nostalgic, flashy, cheeky.
- **Fits:** Poster, UI (retro), Product (fashion, beauty), Brand (Gen Z).
- **Prompt:** `Y2K aesthetic, liquid chrome and iridescent gloss, icy blue and hot pink, sparkles and starbursts, retro browser windows, bubbly techno typography`
- **Misfire:** default hot-pink overload; ask for chrome and blues if the user wants cooler.
- **Pairs:** Neo Frutiger Aero, Pixel Art, Utilitarian · **Clashes:** Wabi Sabi, Neoclassical, Acanthus.

### Neo Frutiger Aero
- **DNA:** aqua blue, fresh green, white, sky gradients · rounded humanist sans · glossy gel buttons, water droplets, bubbles, lens flare · clean, open, sunny · grass, clouds, fish, translucent orbs, early-2000s OS chrome.
- **Mood:** optimistic, fresh, clean-futuristic.
- **Fits:** UI, Poster, Brand (fintech, Web3, wellness tech), Product.
- **Prompt:** `Frutiger Aero aesthetic, glossy aqua and fresh green, translucent bubbles and water droplets, glossy gel buttons, bright sky and grass, rounded humanist sans`
- **Misfire:** dated stock-desktop look; feels sparse. Push density and life if the user wants it current.
- **Pairs:** Glassmorphism, Y2K, Nautical · **Clashes:** Gothic, Dark Magic Academia, Tenebrism.

### Synthwave
- **DNA:** magenta, purple, cyan, sunset orange on black · 3D chrome script and italic display · neon glow, VHS scanlines · horizon with perspective grid, centered sun · wireframe mountains, palm silhouettes, sports cars.
- **Mood:** retro-futuristic, cinematic, nostalgic.
- **Fits:** Poster (music, events), Scene, Product (gaming), Brand.
- **Prompt:** `synthwave, neon magenta and cyan, glowing perspective grid to a striped sunset, chrome 80s script title, VHS scanlines`
- **Misfire:** the stock sunset-grid cliché with nothing specific to the brief.
- **Pairs:** Pixel Art, Cybercore, Vaporwave · **Clashes:** Japandi, Wabi Sabi, Farmhouse / Cottagecore.

### Vaporwave
- **DNA:** pastel pink, lilac, teal, seafoam · Japanese characters, Windows 95 system fonts · VHS glitch, low-fi JPEG · collage on grids or void, floating objects · Greek busts, palm trees, dolphins, old OS windows, checkerboard floors.
- **Mood:** ironic, dreamy, melancholic.
- **Fits:** Poster (music), Illustration, Scene.
- **Prompt:** `vaporwave aesthetic, pastel pink and teal, Greek marble bust, retro OS windows, checkerboard floor, VHS glitch, Japanese katakana text`
- **Misfire:** random meme collage; garbled fake Japanese.
- **Pairs:** Surrealism, Synthwave, Kitsch · **Clashes:** Luxury Typography, Japandi, Utilitarian.

### Cybercore
- **DNA:** black with acid green, electric cyan, warning red · monospace and glitch display · neon glow, chromatic aberration, scanlines · dense HUD overlays, data streams · code rain, circuit lines, hazard symbols, wireframes.
- **Mood:** high-tech, chaotic, dystopian.
- **Fits:** Poster (tech, gaming), UI (HUD), Scene, Character.
- **Prompt:** `cybercore aesthetic, black background with acid green and electric cyan neon, glitch and chromatic aberration, HUD overlays and code streams, monospace type`
- **Misfire:** unreadable clutter; generic "hacker" stock look.
- **Pairs:** Synthwave, Brutalism, Utilitarian · **Clashes:** Coquette, Farmhouse / Cottagecore, Ethereal.

## Bold & Loud

### Memphis
- **DNA:** clashing primaries plus pastel pink, mint, yellow · chunky geometric sans · flat color, terrazzo and squiggle patterns · scattered, asymmetric, rule-breaking · squiggles, zigzags, confetti dots, cylinders and cones.
- **Mood:** playful, rebellious, 80s fun.
- **Fits:** Poster, Brand (creative studios), Illustration, Product (packaging).
- **Prompt:** `Memphis design, scattered geometric shapes, squiggles and zigzags, clashing primaries with pastel pink and mint, flat color, chunky geometric sans`
- **Misfire:** random shape soup; subject lost.
- **Pairs:** Pop Art, Neo-Brutalism, Kawaii · **Clashes:** Japandi, Luxury Typography, Gothic.

### Pop Art
- **DNA:** red, yellow, cyan, black outline · comic display fonts · Ben-Day halftone dots, flat ink · panels, bold crops, repetition grids · speech bubbles, onomatopoeia, celebrity-style portraits.
- **Mood:** loud, energetic, ironic.
- **Fits:** Poster, Product (packaging), Illustration, Character.
- **Prompt:** `pop art style, bold black outlines, Ben-Day halftone dots, flat primary colors, comic speech bubble, punchy cropped composition`
- **Misfire:** copied Lichtenstein/Warhol pastiche; speech-bubble text garbled.
- **Pairs:** Memphis, Kitsch, Rebus · **Clashes:** Ethereal, Wabi Sabi, Japandi.

### Brutalism
- **DNA:** greyscale with one raw accent · system or monospace fonts, oversized · raw concrete, default UI, harsh edges · unbalanced blocks, exposed grid · plain boxes, underlined links, unstyled buttons.
- **Mood:** raw, honest, confrontational.
- **Fits:** Poster (art, counterculture), UI (experimental), Brand.
- **Prompt:** `brutalist graphic design, raw unpolished layout, oversized monospace type, greyscale with one harsh accent color, exposed grid and default UI elements`
- **Misfire:** just looks broken or unfinished rather than deliberate.
- **Pairs:** Utilitarian, Cybercore, Swiss Style · **Clashes:** Coquette, Ethereal, Filigree.

### Neo-Brutalism
- **DNA:** saturated yellow, pink, green, blue on off-white · big bold grotesk, chunky headings · flat fills, thick black outlines, hard offset shadows with zero blur · blocky cards, hard corners, purposeful asymmetry · stickers, arrows, stars, chunky buttons.
- **Mood:** confident, punchy, usable.
- **Fits:** UI, Poster, Brand (startups, creators), Infographic.
- **Prompt:** `neo-brutalism, thick black outlines, hard offset drop shadows with no blur, saturated yellow pink and green blocks, chunky bold grotesk headings, square corners`
- **Misfire:** soft or blurred shadows creep in; rounded corners undo the look.
- **Pairs:** Memphis, Rebus, Y2K · **Clashes:** Ethereal, Glassmorphism, Filigree.

### Graffiti
- **DNA:** neon pink, lime, electric blue over concrete grey · wildstyle and tag lettering, bubble letters · spray paint, drips, overspray, brick · layered, energetic, wall-sized · crowns, arrows, characters, stencils.
- **Mood:** urban, defiant, expressive.
- **Fits:** Poster (music, streetwear), Brand (streetwear), Product (sneakers), Illustration.
- **Prompt:** `graffiti street art, spray-paint texture with drips and overspray, bold wildstyle lettering, neon colors on concrete wall, layered tags and stencils`
- **Misfire:** headline text unreadable once stylized; ask for "legible" bubble letters.
- **Pairs:** Pop Art, Mixed Media, Cybercore · **Clashes:** Luxury Typography, Neoclassical, Japandi.

### Kitsch
- **DNA:** saturated clashing pinks, golds, turquoise · dated ad fonts, script and novelty display · shiny plastic, glitter, velvet, faux gold · crowded, over-the-top · flamingos, souvenirs, sentimental ornaments, retro ad cut-outs.
- **Mood:** campy, ironic, joyful excess.
- **Fits:** Poster (parody, nightlife), Product (Gen Z fashion), Photography (set design).
- **Prompt:** `kitsch aesthetic, deliberately over-the-top, glossy plastic and glitter, clashing pink turquoise and faux gold, retro advertising cut-outs, novelty typography`
- **Misfire:** reads as accidental bad taste instead of knowing irony.
- **Pairs:** Pop Art, Vaporwave, Y2K · **Clashes:** Japandi, Luxury Typography, Minimalism.

## Structured & Functional

### Bauhaus
- **DNA:** red, blue, yellow, black, off-white · geometric sans (Futura-like), lowercase · flat color, print grain · strict grid, asymmetric balance · circles, squares, triangles, bold bars.
- **Mood:** rational, modernist, purposeful.
- **Fits:** Poster, Brand, Infographic, Document.
- **Prompt:** `Bauhaus design, primary red blue and yellow with black, circles squares and triangles, strict asymmetric grid, geometric sans-serif, flat print texture`
- **Misfire:** decorative shape collage with no structure.
- **Pairs:** Swiss Style, Modular Typography, Mid-Century · **Clashes:** Baroque, Coquette, Shabby Chic.

### Utilitarian
- **DNA:** off-white, khaki, grey, black with one sharp accent (safety orange) · monospace and industrial condensed sans, small caps labels · matte paper, stencil, printed spec sheets · tight modular grid, boxed sections · barcodes, serial numbers, technical callouts, warning labels.
- **Mood:** engineered, efficient, no-nonsense.
- **Fits:** UI, Product (gear, apparel), Brand, Document, Breakdown.
- **Prompt:** `utilitarian design, function-first modular grid, boxed sections, monospace and industrial condensed type, muted khaki and grey with one safety-orange accent, barcodes and spec labels`
- **Misfire:** turns into bland corporate; lacks the one sharp accent.
- **Pairs:** Y2K, Brutalism, Swiss Style · **Clashes:** Baroque, Coquette, Shabby Chic.

### Bento Box
- **DNA:** neutral light or dark base with one brand accent · clean modern sans · soft shadows, rounded corners · modular tiles of mixed sizes · icons with labels, stat tiles, small charts, photos in cells.
- **Mood:** organized, friendly, current.
- **Fits:** UI, Infographic, Brand (feature showcase), Poster (product feature grid).
- **Prompt:** `bento grid layout, rounded modular tiles of varied sizes, soft shadows, neutral palette with one accent color, icon and label tiles, clean modern sans-serif`
- **Misfire:** identical tiles with no hierarchy; tiny unreadable labels.
- **Pairs:** Glassmorphism, Neo-Brutalism, Minimalism · **Clashes:** Baroque, Graffiti, Surrealism.

### Modular Typography
- **DNA:** black and white with one or two bold accents · letters built from grid modules, geometric · flat, print-crisp · type as structure, uniform spacing · stacked and interlocking letterforms.
- **Mood:** structural, rhythmic, experimental.
- **Fits:** Poster, Brand (identity systems), Document (covers).
- **Prompt:** `modular typography, geometric letterforms built from grid modules, uniform spacing, stacked interlocking type as the main visual, black white and one bold accent`
- **Misfire:** letters become unreadable; misspellings.
- **Pairs:** Bauhaus, Swiss Style, Neo-Brutalism · **Clashes:** Art Nouveau, Shabby Chic, Ethereal.

### Swiss Style
- **DNA:** white, black, one strong red or blue · neo-grotesk (Helvetica-like), flush-left, ragged-right · flat, photographic or geometric imagery · rigorous grid, asymmetric, strong whitespace · large type blocks, rules, cropped photos.
- **Mood:** objective, precise, modern.
- **Fits:** Poster, Document, Brand, Infographic.
- **Prompt:** `Swiss International Typographic Style, rigorous modular grid, neo-grotesk sans-serif flush left, asymmetric layout, white black and a single red accent, objective photography`
- **Misfire:** generic corporate template; text too small.
- **Pairs:** Bauhaus, Utilitarian, Minimalism · **Clashes:** Baroque, Kitsch, Bohemian.

### Flat Design
- **DNA:** bright solid colors, harmonious set · simple sans · no gradients, no textures, no shadows (or long flat shadows) · clear shapes, centered icons · simplified objects and people, iconography.
- **Mood:** clear, friendly, approachable.
- **Fits:** Infographic, UI, Illustration (explainers), Character.
- **Prompt:** `flat design illustration, solid colors with no gradients or textures, simplified geometric shapes, clean sans-serif labels, clear iconography`
- **Misfire:** looks like generic corporate stock illustration.
- **Pairs:** Bauhaus, Rebus, Bento Box · **Clashes:** Tenebrism, Baroque, Glassmorphism.

## Calm & Natural

### Minimalism
- **DNA:** one or two colors on white or neutral · restrained sans or serif · smooth, matte · vast negative space, one focal element · almost nothing beyond the subject.
- **Mood:** calm, focused, confident.
- **Fits:** any type; strongest for Product, Poster, Brand.
- **Prompt:** `minimalist composition, single focal subject, vast negative space, restrained two-color palette, clean typography, nothing extraneous`
- **Misfire:** empty and characterless (the "make it clean" result). Usually best as the accent to a named style.
- **Pairs:** Wabi Sabi, Japandi, Luxury Typography · **Clashes:** Kitsch, Memphis, Victorian.

### Japandi
- **DNA:** oat, sand, warm grey, charcoal, muted sage · clean light sans · light oak, linen, paper, ceramic · balanced, uncluttered, low furniture lines · simple vessels, plants, wooden objects.
- **Mood:** serene, intentional, warm-minimal.
- **Fits:** Space, Product (home, lifestyle), Brand (wellness), Photography.
- **Prompt:** `Japandi style, light oak and linen, warm neutral oat and charcoal palette, uncluttered balanced composition, natural materials, soft daylight, clean light sans-serif`
- **Misfire:** sterile showroom; too beige to read.
- **Pairs:** Wabi Sabi, Minimalism, Mid-Century · **Clashes:** Memphis, Vaporwave, Graffiti.

### Wabi Sabi
- **DNA:** clay, ash, moss, raw umber, undyed linen · minimal sans or brush accents · rough pottery, plaster, weathered wood, natural shadow · asymmetric, open, imperfect · hand-placed circles, cracked ceramics, single branch, kintsugi.
- **Mood:** humble, quiet, contemplative.
- **Fits:** Product (tea, ceramics, wellness), Space, Photography, Brand, Poster (restrained).
- **Prompt:** `wabi-sabi aesthetic, earthy clay ash and moss tones, rough handmade textures, asymmetric composition with generous negative space, soft natural light, imperfect hand-drawn circles`
- **Misfire:** too sparse; looks unfinished rather than intentional. Add a few tactile details if it feels empty.
- **Pairs:** Minimalism, Japandi, Scrapbook · **Clashes:** Y2K, Cybercore, Neo Frutiger Aero.

### Light Academia
- **DNA:** cream, beige, warm white, light brown · classic book serifs, italic accents · linen, old paper, sunlight through windows · calm, framed, still-life · books, pressed flowers, tea, busts, handwritten notes.
- **Mood:** scholarly, gentle, romantic.
- **Fits:** Photography, Poster (book clubs, study), Product (stationery), Illustration.
- **Prompt:** `light academia aesthetic, creamy neutrals and warm beige, sunlit library still life, old books and linen textures, classic serif typography`
- **Misfire:** bland sepia; no focal object.
- **Pairs:** Scrapbook, Ethereal, Neoclassical · **Clashes:** Cybercore, Neo-Brutalism, Pop Art.

## Dark & Dramatic

### Tenebrism
- **DNA:** near-black with warm candle gold and flesh tones · sparse classic serif · oil paint, glaze, deep shadow · single hard light source, subject emerging from darkness · spotlit faces, hands, still lifes.
- **Mood:** intense, cinematic, solemn.
- **Fits:** Photography, Poster (film), Scene, Product (spirits, luxury).
- **Prompt:** `tenebrism, extreme chiaroscuro, single hard light source, subject emerging from deep black shadow, oil-painting texture, warm gold highlights`
- **Misfire:** crushed blacks lose the subject; muddy.
- **Pairs:** Baroque, Gothic, Surrealism · **Clashes:** Flat Design, Kawaii, Neo Frutiger Aero.

### Gothic
- **DNA:** black, deep purple, blood red, stone grey · blackletter · stained glass, carved stone, candle smoke · vertical, pointed arches, symmetrical · cathedrals, ravens, roses, wrought iron.
- **Mood:** dark, mysterious, devout.
- **Fits:** Poster (music, fantasy), Document (book covers), Illustration, Scene.
- **Prompt:** `gothic aesthetic, pointed arches and stained glass, blackletter typography, deep purple black and blood red, candlelit stone, vertical symmetrical composition`
- **Misfire:** Halloween-store cheapness.
- **Pairs:** Tenebrism, Dark Magic Academia, Victorian · **Clashes:** Kawaii, Neo Frutiger Aero, Memphis.

### Dark Magic Academia
- **DNA:** black, oxblood, antique gold, candle amber · calligraphic serifs · aged parchment, leather, wax, etching · cluttered study, layered still life · spellbooks, candles, astrolabes, keys, alchemical diagrams.
- **Mood:** mysterious, scholarly, occult.
- **Fits:** Poster (fantasy, book clubs), Product (candles, witchy brands), Scene, Character.
- **Prompt:** `dark magic academia, candlelit study with spellbooks and astrolabes, aged parchment and leather, black oxblood and antique gold, calligraphic serif, antique etching details`
- **Misfire:** derivative wizard-school imagery.
- **Pairs:** Gothic, Victorian, Mystical Western · **Clashes:** Neo Frutiger Aero, Kawaii, Flat Design.

### Mystical Western
- **DNA:** terracotta, sand, burnt orange, midnight blue, gold · western slab serifs and ornate frames · leather, tooled metal, dusty grain · centered tarot-card framing · cacti, snakes, sun and moon faces, eyes, stars, cow skulls.
- **Mood:** mystical, rugged, folkloric.
- **Fits:** Poster (music), Product (crystals, candles), Brand (tarot apps), Illustration.
- **Prompt:** `mystical western, tarot-card framing, desert cacti with sun and moon faces, celestial symbols, terracotta and midnight blue with gold, western slab serif`
- **Misfire:** costume-party cowboy; disrespectful cultural motifs.
- **Pairs:** South West / Wild West, Dark Magic Academia, Art Nouveau · **Clashes:** Bento Box, Glassmorphism, Neo Frutiger Aero.

### Steampunk
- **DNA:** brass, copper, mahogany, sepia · Victorian serifs and engraved type · riveted metal, leather, glass, steam · intricate mechanical clutter · gears, pressure gauges, goggles, airships, pipes.
- **Mood:** inventive, adventurous, vintage-futuristic.
